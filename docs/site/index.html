Privacy Policy – Media Monitor

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Media Monitor ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that lets you categorize web pages and save them to a Google Spreadsheet in your own Google Drive.

In short: the developer does not collect, store, sell, or share any of your data. The extension has no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the extension accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Title and URL of the active tabRead only when you open the extension and choose to save the page, so it can be recorded under a category you selected.Written to a spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
Google account authorization (OAuth token)To let the extension create and write to spreadsheets on your behalf. Only the drive.file scope is requested, which limits access to files the extension itself creates.Held by Chrome; used only to call Google APIs directly from your browser.
Projects and categories you defineTo remember your settings between sessions.Stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) and in your spreadsheet.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or have access to your data. All data stays in your browser and in your own Google account.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or for creditworthiness or lending purposes.
  • We do not run remotely hosted code.

Third-party services

The extension communicates directly with Google APIs (Google Drive and Google Sheets) from your browser to store your data. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • activeTab – read the title and URL of the current tab when you use the extension.
  • storage – save your projects and categories locally.
  • identity – sign in with your Google account to access your spreadsheet.

Your control

You can delete the saved data at any time by removing the spreadsheet from your Google Drive, uninstalling the extension (which clears local storage), or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
